## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide editorial website hero for EMILIA, an AI workforce marketplace; copy will be separate from the image.
Primary request: nature-related, calming, quietly inspiring, not futuristic. A beautiful real-feeling coastal meadow at first light, a narrow natural walking path through soft grasses toward a tranquil ocean horizon. Mature wind-shaped coastal trees frame one side, the meadow rolls gently down to a secluded cove. Soft sunlight falls through the branches onto the path. The feeling is breathing room, possibility, and knowing where you want to go.
Style/medium: exceptional fine-art landscape photography, photorealistic, restrained editorial color grade, natural irregular textures, understated luxury. Visually arresting through light and composition, not spectacle. Something you would genuinely want to pause and look at.
Composition: panoramic 16:9 landscape, layered foreground grasses, path and gentle middle-distance coastline, wide calm sky and sea. Keep the central path and ocean view legible in a centered 4:3 mobile crop. A few grasses softly out of focus in the foreground; main vista gently detailed. Natural asymmetry, no perfect symmetry.
Lighting/mood: pale early-morning sunlight, soft diffuse haze at the horizon, calm weather. Warm ivory sky, muted sage and olive vegetation, flax and pale gold grasses, soft slate-blue ocean. Airy with rich readable detail, not bleached out, not dark, not orange.
Text: none.
Avoid: buildings, futuristic architecture, robots, hands, technology, holograms, gates, fantasy, alien landscapes, neon colors, intense sunset, stormy skies, dramatic jagged cliffs, oversaturation, stock-photo lens flare, illustrated look, logos, watermarks. No people needed. This is a calm, real-world nature image, not a destination advertisement or evidence of an EMILIA location.
